08

What people ask before the call.

My agency says the tracking is fine. Why check?

Ask for two screens: Purchase showing as Deduplicated, and the Event Match Quality of your Purchase. If they show both, great. If not, the 15-min call shows you where you are.

Does it work with Google Ads too?

Yes. Google Ads gets the same sale from your server, with the click id (gclid, gbraid or wbraid), the hashed e-mail and phone, and the order id so it counts once. If you only run Meta, I fix Meta only.

Does it work with Shopify, Hotmart, Kiwify or Eduzz?

The audit is the same on any checkout. On an own checkout I fix all of it. On hosted checkouts the fix goes as far as each platform lets you configure, and I tell you what is possible in the audit, before we go on.

How long does it take?

3 business days after you give access to your Meta account, Google Ads and your checkout. I send you a step by step for the access.

Will my sales count twice?

No. Both roads send the same sale with the same order id, and each platform keeps one. On Meta that is what Deduplicated means.

What happens with my customers' data?

Buyer data is normalized and hashed with SHA-256 before it leaves your server, the way Meta and Google ask.

Do I need to change my checkout?

No. The tags go on your pages and the server part goes next to the checkout you already run. Your buyer doesn't see any change, except a faster checkout when Meta is slow.

Can I see the code?

The audit and the proof are public on GitHub. The code is private for now, and I walk through it and run it live on a call.
