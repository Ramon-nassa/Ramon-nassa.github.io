03

Five steps, and the last one goes back to the ad.

It plugs into the landing page, form and CRM you already use. Nothing to rebuild.

  1. 01 · capture

    Lead comes in

    Any page form, Meta Lead Ads or Google lead forms. One script line keeps gclid, fbclid and UTMs for 90 days.

  2. 02 · clean

    Dedupe and enrich

    Same person twice is one record and one message. Company data from the website and AI, or Clay.

  3. 03 · score

    AI scores the fit

    Your rules plus an AI rubric give a score and a confidence level. Call now, nurture, or a person reviews it.

  4. 04 · CRM

    Sales gets it in seconds

    Lead lands in HubSpot or your CRM with a first reply written in your business's own words.

  5. 05 · signal

    Qualified goes back

    Meta gets it through the Conversions API, Google Ads through the Data Manager API. Same event_id, nothing counts twice.

Step 5 is what changes the campaign. The algorithm starts learning who becomes a qualified lead, not just who fills a form.

07

What people ask before the call.

Will my CPL go up?

It can, and that's ok. The platform stops buying the cheapest form fills. Watch the cost per qualified lead and the CPA instead. Those are the ones that should go down.

How long does it take?

3 business days after you give access to your ad accounts, CRM and form. Most of that access takes a few minutes on your side, and I send you a step by step.

Do I need to rebuild my landing page or change my CRM?

No. On the page it's one script line, and your form keeps working like before. HubSpot is built in. Other CRMs connect through their own API.

What happens with my customers' data?

Contact data only leaves the engine hashed with SHA-256. A signal is sent only for a qualified lead who gave ad consent.

What if Meta or Google is down?

The send is retried and goes out once. If the outage is longer than the retry window, the lead is parked and resumed later, without repeating the steps that already worked.

Is AI going to change my ads or my budget?

No. The AI scores the lead and writes the first reply. It doesn't touch your campaigns or your budget. And when it isn't sure about a lead, it doesn't guess. The lead goes to a person to review.

Can I see the code?

The proof is public on GitHub. The code repo is private for now, and I walk through it and run it live on a call.
